On your day

A letter, for you

Dear Amma,

You've never asked me for anything. Not once. Not a gift, not a thank you, not even for me to notice. So I'm noticing now, on paper, where you can't change the subject.

You gave up your quiet mornings, your rest, your own plans — and you did it so casually that for years I thought that was just what mothers were. It isn't. That was you. That was always you, choosing me, over and over, without ever calling it a sacrifice.

Everything decent in me came from watching you. The patience I'm still learning, the way I keep going when things get hard, the fact that I care about people at all — all of it is yours first.

You once said your favourite thing in the world is simply being with me. I want you to know it's mine too. It always was.

So happy birthday, Amma. I hope this year is gentle with you. I hope somebody worries about you for a change. And I hope you know that no matter how old I get or how far I go, I'm still the boy who just wants to sit next to you.

— Your son, Charan 25 August · with all my love
